Problem Statement for Hackathon 3
Title: The Autonomous Supply Chain & Executive Control Plane
Date: Wednesday, 9th-11th September 2026 
Theme: "Ship, Secure, and Scale: From Predictive Insights to Production-Ready Automation."
Objective: Teams will transition their Stage 2 analytical prototypes into hardened, deployable, AI-powered production services. The goal is to prove that the solution can survive the rigors of KPC’s live downstream operations, integrate seamlessly with existing workflows, and deliver a quantifiable Return on Investment (ROI) to executive leadership.
Deliverables for Stage 3:
Production CI/CD & Monitoring: The solution must be deployed via a mature CI/CD pipeline to a live, monitored environment with automated alerting (e.g., PagerDuty/Slack integrations for system failures or threshold breaches).
The Executive Control Plane: A polished, executive-facing dashboard that translates complex operational data into strategic business metrics (e.g., "KES Saved in Demurrage," "Throughput % Increase").
Handover & Support Readiness: Comprehensive documentation, including an operations manual, disaster recovery plan, and a formal support handover plan for the Em-Tech/KPC IT teams.
The ROI Business Case: A rigorously quantified financial narrative detailing the implementation costs versus the operational savings (e.g., reduced truck turnaround time, prevented stock-outs).
Presentation: A 10-minute live demo and ROI pitch + extended Q&A, pitched directly to KPC and Em-Tech executive leadership.

OUR CHOSEN DOMAIN:
Domain 3: Downstream Refinery & Pipeline Integration
Focus: Synchronizing the physical flow of product from the refinery output to the regional depots.
Downstream Demand Forecasting & Inventory Replenishment: Build a predictive inventory model that anticipates regional fuel demand spikes and automatically triggers pipeline batching or truck dispatch to maintain optimal stock levels.
